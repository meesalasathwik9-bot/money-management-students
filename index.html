Start with one simple habit.

Managing money doesn't begin with having a lot of money. It begins with understanding where your money goes and making thoughtful decisions.

Know where your money goes

Whenever you receive money, take a moment to think about how you want to use it. Spending without a plan can make it difficult to understand where your money went.

A simple habit of tracking your expenses can help you notice patterns, make better choices and become more aware of your spending.

Small step: Write down your expenses for one week. At the end of the week, review what you spent and what you could plan better next time.
