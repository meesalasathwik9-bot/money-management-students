Introduction · Preview

Small decisions can shape your money habits.

Managing money isn't only about how much you earn. It's also about understanding the choices you make with what you have. For students, those choices might be deciding whether to buy something today, save a little for later, or plan ahead for an expense.

Learning these habits doesn't require a big income or complicated spreadsheets. Start by noticing your spending, setting one simple goal, and giving yourself time to make thoughtful decisions. Small steps, repeated regularly, can help you feel more confident about money.

“You don't need to be rich to start managing money well. You need a starting point—and a habit.”
